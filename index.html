Eigo Jouzu

This is a collection of games I have created to supplement English language education in Japan.

The games are primarily targeted at middle/junior high school students, but can be used for elementary and high school students too.

For full disclosure, some games were partly or fully made using AI. Not all games were made using AI. They have been checked and tested to make sure there are no issues.

Using these games in schools may require whitelisting - ask your IT department to wildcard whitelist this domain, as it should be possible.
